About & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first meeting.

1. Where do you meet?

We meet in the Wellness Center on campus.

2. What day and time is the club?

We meet every Friday during lunch, starting at 11:50 AM.

3. What do you guys do at meetings?

We hang out in a welcoming environment to celebrate and learn about cultures from all across East Africa. We also discuss current issues affecting East Africa and plan ways we can raise awareness and contribute.

4. Do I have to be East African to join?

Everyone is welcome! Whether you are from East Africa or just want to learn more and support the community, feel free to pull up.
